How GDS Merit Selection Operates

Understanding Karnataka circle vacancy allocation and cutoff trends

1. 10th Standard Marks Only

There is no written exam or interview. Selection is generated automatically in order of merit from 10th board marks aggregated to 4 decimal places.

2. Division & Category Preference

Candidates can apply to multiple posts within their chosen division. UR seats are open to all, while reserved seats require valid caste/category certificates.

3. Estimated Chance Analysis

Our tool evaluates your marks against Karnataka's 1,115 vacancies and historical baseline cutoffs to highlight divisions with the highest probability.
